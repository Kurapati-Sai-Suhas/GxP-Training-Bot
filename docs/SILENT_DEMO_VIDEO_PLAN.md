# Silent Demo Video — Captions and Shot List

No narration. On-screen text carries the explanation. Target **5–7 minutes**.

**The rule for a silent demo: hold every key screen for at least 5 seconds.** What feels slow while
recording reads as comfortable on playback. Rushing is the single most common failure.

---

## HOW TO BUILD IT

1. **Record the screen segments** in order (list below), with no talking.
2. **Add the title cards afterwards** in Clipchamp (built into Windows — search "Clipchamp"):
   `+ Add media` → `Text` → pick **Plain** or **Basic Lower Third** → type the caption → drag onto
   the timeline between clips.
3. Optionally add quiet background music at **10–15% volume**. Clipchamp → `Music & SFX`. Keep it
   neutral; skip it entirely if unsure — silence is fine for an academic submission.

**Title cards: 3 seconds each. Screen segments: as listed.**

# RECORDING NOTES FOR A SILENT VIDEO

- **Move the mouse slowly and deliberately.** With no narration, the cursor is the pointer — jerky
  movement is disorienting.
- **Never leave a screen before it can be read.** Count to five in your head.
- **Browser zoom 125–150%.** Text that's fine on your monitor is unreadable on a phone, and there's
  no voice to compensate.
- **Terminal font 18pt+.**
- **Don't record typing.** Have commands ready to paste, or type them before recording and just
  press Enter on camera.
- **No system sounds** — mute notifications. Even in a silent video, a notification chime is jarring.
